How an order runs on Luna

From a brief to a piece ready to sell
  1. Send a briefA sketch, a photo, a swatch or one line about what you want, and how many, even if it is one.
  2. Choose by rolePick the cloth with the name of who made it, then the hands for each stage. One partner or several.
    FabricDesignerWeaverDyerEmbroideryStitching
  3. Get an instant quoteReal fabric prices with the making in it, every line shown, as a quote link your team can accept. Nothing starts until you do.
  4. Approve a sample, follow it liveEvery stage is a task on the maker's phone, with photos as it goes. You follow it in your account.
  5. Receive it ready to sellEvery piece arrives with what you need to list it, its story and provenance attached.
    MakersFabric supplierCraft storyPhotosEnergy & water*

Every partner works in the Luna partner app

On Android and iPhone, partners accept work, start and finish each stage, upload photos and video, book courier pickups and see what they are owed. Each partner is paid for their own stage once it is recorded and approved.
