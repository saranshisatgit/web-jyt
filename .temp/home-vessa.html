FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is provenance actually verified?

Every piece carries a Digital Product Passport linking artisan, village, material lot and certification — auditable on the product page, EU ESPR-ready.

Do makers need a website?

No. Their storefront, payments and shipping are switched on day one, on their own domain, from a phone-first portal.

Who holds the margin?

The maker. Buyers deal direct — the platform takes a small commission, not a chain of agents.

Can I commission a custom piece?

Yes — describe it, pick an atelier, and track it from sketch to shipment.
